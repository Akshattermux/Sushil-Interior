Clear Guidance

Frequently asked questions
about kitchen appliances & cutlery.

How do I choose between an engineered quartz slab and Black Galaxy granite? +

Engineered quartz provides a non-porous, perfectly uniform surface that is 100% impervious to haldi (turmeric), lemon juice, and tea stains. Natural Black Galaxy granite offers higher direct scorching resistance up to 500°C and a classic shimmering mica aesthetic. Both are fabricated and calibrated to exact dimensions in our workshop.

Are the solid CP teak cutlery trays safe from moisture and kitchen steam? +

Yes. Every CP Teak cutlery tray undergoes 45 days of scientific kiln drying to an 8–10% moisture content. They are then treated with food-safe natural walnut and mineral oil, creating a water-repellent barrier that prevents mould, swelling, or odor absorption.

Do you provide on-site measurement and white-glove installation? +

Yes, absolutely. We provide complimentary on-site laser measurements across Saharanpur and Dehradun. Our master Karigars personally route the sink and stove hob openings, install the countertop slabs, and mount all German drawer runners.

What warranty applies to built-in hobs, chimneys, and Blum hardware? +

All built-in glass hobs carry a 2 to 5-year burner and glass warranty. Chimneys feature lifetime motor warranties. German Blum and Hettich drawer runners and soft-close hinges come with lifetime replacement warranties.
