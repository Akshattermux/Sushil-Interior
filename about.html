For over twenty-five years, Sushil Interior has stood at the crossroads of Saharanpur’s 400-year-old carving lineage and state-of-the-art turnkey interior architecture. We transform seasoned CP Teak and Sheesham into living sanctuaries.

The Saharanpur Heritage

The Wood City of India

Saharanpur, situated at the foothills of the Shivalik Himalayas, is globally celebrated as India's preeminent center for master wooden craftsmanship. Here, woodworking is not merely a manufacturing trade; it is a sacred inherited art form passed from father to son across generations.

At Sushil Interior, we preserve the soul of the traditional Saharanpur Ustaad hand-carving while integrating modern German CNC joinery, moisture-controlled kiln chambers, and high-performance Italian PU finishes.

The 4-Pillar Quality Protocol

Our Woodcraft Standard

01 / SCIENTIFIC SEASONING

Kiln-Dried Moisture Control

All raw Central Province (CP) Teak and Indian Sheesham logs undergo 45 days in our thermal kiln chambers, reducing moisture to a strict 8–10%. This guarantees zero warping or cracking across Indian monsoons and dry summers.

02 / HAND CHISELLED

Generational Karigari

Our master artisans hand-carve geometric jaali patterns, fluted reliefs, and solid wood joinery using hand gouges and chisels that bring organic warmth into contemporary minimalism.

03 / MODERN MODULAR

German Hardware Integration

For modular kitchens and sliding wardrobes, we pair traditional hardwoods with German Hettich and Blum soft-close runners, hydraulic lift-ups, and anti-termite marine ply bases.

04 / WHITE GLOVE

Insured White-Glove Setup

From our Saharanpur workshop and Dehradun design studio, each piece is dispatched in moisture-proof timber crates with white-glove on-site installation by our own master Karigars.
